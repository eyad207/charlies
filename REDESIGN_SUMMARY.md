## 🎨 Design System

### **Colors**

- **Primary**: #FDB714 (Golden Yellow)
- **Secondary**: #F5A612, #E5A613 (Gold variants)
- **Accent**: Orange, Red gradients
- **Dark**: Black, Gray-900
- **Light**: White, Gray-50

### **Typography**

- **Headings**: 4xl-8xl, font-black (900 weight)
- **Body**: Base-2xl, font-medium/semibold
- **Small**: xs-sm, font-semibold

### **Spacing**

- Generous padding (py-20 to py-32)
- Clear section separation
- Breathing room around content

### **Shadows**

- Soft shadows: shadow-lg, shadow-xl
- Glowing shadows: shadow-[0_0_40px_rgba(...)]
- Layered depth
